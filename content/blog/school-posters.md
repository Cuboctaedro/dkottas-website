One of the most common complaints designers have is being asked to do free work for friends and relatives. However, this type of work has its own merits. Depending on the cicumstances it can become a loved project although it was something small.

For the last two years i was activelly involved with the Parents' Association at my son's school. It was something that I enjoyed doing. Part of my work there was to create posters for various events. Usually it was a last minute decision and the plan was to print digitally a handfull of copies and use the image on social media. Sure it was not the type of project that every designer dreams. But it was fun.
